Established in 2006

Construction & Maintenance Built on Experience

M.J.CO Limited is a general contractor and maintenance company based in Trinidad and Tobago. Since 2006, the company has undertaken industrial, commercial and residential projects, including building works, renovations and property maintenance.

Who We Are

Our team combines skilled workmanship with practical field experience across homes, commercial spaces, warehouses, and industrial facilities.

We take pride in helping clients complete projects that are safe, durable, clean, and built to last.

How We Work

  • Clear site visits, scope review, and practical recommendations.
  • Professional quotation support including BOQs where required.
  • Hands-on supervision, schedule management, and safe execution.
  • Strong closeout standards with quality checks and housekeeping.

Our Mission

To provide quality construction services at fair, competitive prices, with professionalism, integrity, honesty and fairness in our relationships with customers, suppliers and subcontractors.

Our Commitment

We aim to build a sustainable business that delivers quality work at reasonable prices and creates ongoing employment opportunities.

Our services extend from site analysis and feasibility studies to project delivery, maintenance and specialist support.
